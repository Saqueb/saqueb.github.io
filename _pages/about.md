I work in the field of observational cosmology and radio astronomy. My work centers on 21-cm Intensity Mapping, where redshifted neutral hydrogen emission is used to trace the Large-scale structures of the universe. Currently, I am working with uGMRT and NenuFar telescope data, and I am also keen to work with next-generation facilities such as SKA-Mid.
